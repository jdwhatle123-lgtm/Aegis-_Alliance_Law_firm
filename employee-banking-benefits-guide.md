## 💡 **FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS**

### **Q: Is there really no cost for banking services?**
**A:** Correct! Whatley National absorbs all banking costs as part of your employment benefits. No monthly fees, no transaction fees, no ATM fees.

### **Q: How safe is the cryptocurrency option?**
**A:** Extremely safe. Your crypto is stored in cold storage with bank-level security. You can allocate 5-25% of your paycheck and change allocation anytime.

### **Q: Can I change my direct deposit settings?**
**A:** Yes, you can modify your allocation percentages, account preferences, or cryptocurrency selections with 30 days notice.

### **Q: What if I need emergency access to funds?**
**A:** Call our 24/7 emergency line at (212) 555-0911. We can provide same-day access to emergency funds up to $5,000.

### **Q: Do I have to bank with Whatley National?**
**A:** No, but you'll miss out on significant benefits. You can opt for direct deposit to another bank, but won't receive the zero-fee banking, early pay, or cryptocurrency options.
