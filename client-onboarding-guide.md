## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: What makes Aegis & Alliance different from other law firms?**
A: We operate under the Whatley Sovereignty Dynasty, providing not just legal representation but sovereign-level advocacy. Our universal defense coverage spans all legal domains, and our interdisciplinary teams ensure comprehensive protection of your rights, reputation, and legacy.

**Q: How quickly can you respond to legal emergencies?**
A: Our emergency response team is available 24/7, with attorney dispatch within 2 hours for urgent situations. For immediate consultations, our emergency hotline is answered within minutes.

**Q: Do you handle matters outside your local area?**
A: Yes, we are licensed in multiple jurisdictions and have attorneys admitted to practice in federal courts nationwide. We can represent clients in any state through local counsel associations and pro hac vice admissions.

**Q: What if my legal matter involves multiple practice areas?**
A: This is our specialty. Our interdisciplinary teams collaborate across practice areas to provide comprehensive protection. You'll have specialists in each relevant area working together under unified leadership.

### Service Questions

**Q: How do you determine which attorneys work on my case?**
A: We assign teams based on the specific legal domains involved, the complexity of the matter, and your individual needs. You'll always have a lead advocate as your primary contact, supported by specialists as needed.

**Q: Can I speak directly with the attorneys working on my case?**
A: Absolutely. While your case manager handles routine communications, you have direct access to your lead advocate and can request consultations with any team member.

**Q: How often will you update me on my case?**
A: You'll receive weekly Dynasty Reports, and we maintain continuous communication through our client portal. For active matters, updates may be daily or even hourly as developments occur.

**Q: What happens if I need legal help while traveling?**
A: Our mobile app provides 24/7 access to emergency legal assistance regardless of your location. We can coordinate with local counsel anywhere in the world.

### Financial Questions

**Q: How do your monthly retainer packages work?**
A: Monthly retainers provide specified services for a predictable monthly fee. Services beyond the package scope are billed at our standard hourly rates. We'll always notify you before exceeding package limits.

**Q: Do you offer payment plans for large legal matters?**
A: Yes, we work with clients to develop payment arrangements that meet their financial needs while ensuring consistent legal representation.

**Q: What happens to unused retainer funds?**
A: Unused monthly retainer funds do not roll over, but they ensure priority access and reduced rates for all services. Annual retainers may have different terms based on the specific agreement.

**Q: Are there any hidden fees or costs?**
A: No. We provide transparent billing with detailed explanations of all charges. Costs are estimated in advance, and you're notified before any significant expenses are incurred.

### Technology Questions

**Q: How secure is the client portal?**
A: Our portal uses military-grade encryption and multi-factor authentication. All data is stored in secure, regularly audited cloud infrastructure with comprehensive backup systems.

**Q: Can I access my information from any device?**
A: Yes, the portal and mobile app work on all devices. You can control which devices have access and can remotely disable access if a device is lost or stolen.

**Q: What if I'm not comfortable with technology?**
A: We provide comprehensive training and support for all technology platforms. Traditional communication methods (phone, email, in-person meetings) remain fully available.

**Q: How long do you keep my information?**
A: We maintain client information according to legal requirements and professional standards, typically 7-10 years after matter conclusion. Information destruction follows secure protocols.
