## 1. STATE BAR LICENSING REQUIREMENTS

### 1.1 Primary Jurisdiction Requirements (Tier 1)

#### NEW YORK STATE BAR
- **Application Requirements:**
  - J.D. from ABA-accredited law school
  - Pass New York Bar Examination (UBE Score: 266+)
  - Character & Fitness Investigation
  - MPRE Score: 85+
- **Annual Requirements:**
  - CLE: 24 hours (4 hours ethics/professionalism)
  - Bar dues: $375/year
  - Registration renewal: Biennial
- **Specialization Opportunities:**
  - Criminal Law Certification
  - Family Law Certification
  - Estate Planning Certification

#### CALIFORNIA STATE BAR
- **Application Requirements:**
  - J.D. from ABA-accredited school OR completion of law office study
  - Pass California Bar Examination
  - Moral Character Determination
  - MPRE Score: 86+
- **Annual Requirements:**
  - MCLE: 25 hours (4 hours legal ethics, 1 hour elimination of bias)
  - Bar dues: $449/year (active status)
  - State Bar Court proceedings compliance
- **Specialized Certifications:**
  - Criminal Law Specialist
  - Family Law Specialist
  - Immigration & Nationality Law
  - Workers' Compensation Law

#### DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA BAR
- **Application Requirements:**
  - J.D. from ABA-accredited law school
  - Bar exam or admission by motion (5+ years practice)
  - Character & Fitness review
  - MPRE Score: 75+
- **Annual Requirements:**
  - CLE: 15 hours (2.5 hours ethics)
  - Bar dues: $285/year
  - Client Protection Fund fee
- **Practice Areas:**
  - Federal regulatory practice
  - Government contracts
  - International law

#### FLORIDA BAR
- **Application Requirements:**
  - J.D. degree
  - Florida Bar Examination (UBE Score: 266+)
  - Character & Fitness Investigation
  - MPRE Score: 80+
- **Annual Requirements:**
  - CLE: 30 hours (5 hours ethics, 2 hours bias elimination, 3 hours mental health/substance abuse)
  - Bar dues: $289/year
  - Technology CLE: 3 hours
- **Board Certifications Available:**
  - Criminal Trial Law
  - Family Law
  - Immigration & Nationality Law
  - Tax Law
  - Real Estate Law

#### TEXAS STATE BAR
- **Application Requirements:**
  - J.D. from approved law school
  - Texas Bar Examination (UBE Score: 270+)
  - Character & Fitness Investigation
  - MPRE Score: 85+
- **Annual Requirements:**
  - MCLE: 15 hours (1 hour ethics)
  - Bar dues: $235/year
  - Client Security Fund fee
- **Specialization Programs:**
  - Criminal Law
  - Family Law
  - Personal Injury Trial Law
  - Tax Law
  - Immigration Law

## 2. FEDERAL COURT ADMISSIONS

### 2.1 U.S. Supreme Court
- **Requirements:**
  - 3+ years state bar admission
  - Sponsorship by two Supreme Court members
  - Application fee: $200
  - Motion for admission

### 2.2 U.S. Courts of Appeals
- **General Requirements:**
  - State bar admission in good standing
  - Application with sponsoring attorney
  - Application fees: $25-50 per circuit

#### Circuit-Specific Admissions:
- **First Circuit** (ME, MA, NH, RI, PR)
- **Second Circuit** (CT, NY, VT)
- **Third Circuit** (DE, NJ, PA, VI)
- **Fourth Circuit** (MD, NC, SC, VA, WV)
- **Fifth Circuit** (LA, MS, TX)
- **Sixth Circuit** (KY, MI, OH, TN)
- **Seventh Circuit** (IL, IN, WI)
- **Eighth Circuit** (AR, IA, MN, MO, NE, ND, SD)
- **Ninth Circuit** (AK, AZ, CA, HI, ID, MT, NV, OR, WA, Guam, N. Mariana Islands)
- **Tenth Circuit** (CO, KS, NM, OK, UT, WY)
- **Eleventh Circuit** (AL, FL, GA)
- **D.C. Circuit** (Washington, D.C.)
- **Federal Circuit** (Patent/IP appeals)

### 2.3 U.S. District Courts
- **Standard Requirements:**
  - Local state bar admission
  - Local rule compliance
  - CLE requirements (varies by district)
  - Application fees: $25-188

### 2.4 Specialized Federal Courts

#### U.S. Tax Court
- **Requirements:**
  - J.D. and state bar admission
  - Application with references
  - Fee: $30
  - Ongoing CLE: 10 hours/year

#### U.S. Court of International Trade
- **Requirements:**
  - State bar admission
  - International trade law experience preferred
  - Application with supporting documents

#### U.S. Court of Federal Claims
- **Requirements:**
  - State bar admission
  - Government contracts experience beneficial
  - Standard federal court application

## 6. COMPLIANCE & REGULATORY

### 6.1 Attorney Trust Account Requirements

#### IOLTA Accounts (Interest on Lawyers Trust Accounts)
- **Federal Requirements:** Bank Secrecy Act compliance
- **State Requirements:** Vary by jurisdiction
- **Record Keeping:** Detailed transaction records
- **Reporting:** Annual IOLTA reports

#### Client Trust Account Management
- **Segregation:** Client funds separate from firm funds
- **Record Keeping:** Individual client ledgers
- **Reconciliation:** Monthly bank reconciliations
- **Audit Requirements:** Annual accountant reviews

### 6.2 Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

#### Customer Due Diligence (CDD)
- **Client Identification:** Beneficial ownership verification
- **Risk Assessment:** Enhanced due diligence for high-risk clients
- **Ongoing Monitoring:** Transaction monitoring systems
- **Reporting:** Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs)

#### FinCEN Requirements
- **CDD Rule Compliance:** Effective May 2018
- **Beneficial Ownership:** 25%+ ownership verification
- **Record Retention:** 5-year minimum
- **Training Requirements:** Staff AML training

### 6.3 Data Privacy Compliance

#### GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
- **Application:** EU residents' data
- **Requirements:**
  - Consent mechanisms
  - Data subject rights
  - Privacy impact assessments
  - Data protection officer (if required)

#### CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- **Application:** California residents
- **Requirements:**
  - Privacy notice requirements
  - Consumer rights implementation
  - Data mapping and inventory
  - Vendor agreements

#### State Privacy Laws
- **Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act**
- **Colorado Privacy Act**
- **Connecticut Data Privacy Act**
- **Other emerging state laws**
